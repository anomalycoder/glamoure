# Glamouré Typography Hierarchy Reference

This reference outlines the exact typographic scale and font pairings to apply for editorial beauty layouts.

## Font Family Stack

| Role | Font Family | Fallback | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Editorial Headings** | `Montserrat` | `sans-serif` | Weight 300 (Light), uppercase, letter-spacing `3.5px` to `4px`. |
| **Flourish Initials & Signatures** | `Alex Brush` | `cursive` | Fluid script, scale `1.35x` to `1.45x`, offset Y aligned with baseline. |
| **Editorial Serif Card Titles** | `Playfair Display` | `serif` | Weight 400, regular or italic, used on photo overlays. |
| **System Body, UI, & Metadata** | `Plus Jakarta Sans` | `sans-serif` | Weight 400 (normal) or 500 (medium), natural casing, clean readability. |
